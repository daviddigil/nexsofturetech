# Nexsofture Tech: Brand Guidelines

> Bold lime energy, deep-blue confidence, glass-like depth. A live version is at `/en/brand/`.

## 1. Logo
- **Mark:** four rounded glass triangles forming an **X**: Products, Engineering, Design and Growth meeting at one point.
- **Lockup:** the mark plus **NEXSOFTURE** (heavy, condensed caps) over **TECH** (wide-tracked).
- **Clear space:** ½ × the width of the mark on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** mark 24 px / 8 mm; full lockup 120 px / 30 mm.
- **Approved versions:** navy-on-lime (primary), lime-on-navy (dark), mark-only (app icons and avatars).
- **Don't:** stretch, rotate, recolour, outline, add drop shadows (except the official 3D glass render), or place it on busy photos without a solid plate.
- **Files:** `public/brand/nexsofture-mark.svg`, `nexsofture-app-icon.svg`, `nexsofture-logo-lime.jpg`

## 2. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Volt Lime** | `#CCFF00` | 204 255 0 | Primary accent, CTAs, highlights (about 10–20% of a layout) |
| **Royal Blue** | `#2B2BFF` | 43 43 255 | Logo glass, links, focus states |
| **Electric Blue** | `#3D5AFE` | 61 90 254 | Gradients, illustrations |
| **Midnight Navy** | `#0B0F3A` | 11 15 58 | Wordmark, headings, body text |
| **Ink** | `#05061A` | 5 6 26 | Dark sections, footer |
| **Cloud** | `#F5F5F7` | 245 245 247 | Light surfaces |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | | Base |

**Contrast:** navy on lime = 15.5:1 ✅ · navy on white = 18:1 ✅ · lime on ink = 17:1 ✅ · never put **lime text on white** ❌.

## 3. Typography
| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Saira** 700–800 | Tight tracking (-2%), line-height 0.95 |
| Body / UI | **Inter** 400–600 | 16–18 px body, line-height 1.6 |
| Arabic | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** 400–700 | No letter-spacing, slightly larger size |

All are free Google Fonts. The website self-hosts them through `next/font`.

## 4. Voice & tone
- **Confident:** say what we build, plainly. No "revolutionary", "synergy" or "cutting-edge".
- **Clear:** answer first, details second. Short sentences.
- **Future-focused:** optimistic about technology, practical about outcomes.
- **Bilingual by default:** Arabic copy is written, not machine-translated.

Tagline: **"Building the next future of software."** / **"نبني المستقبل القادم للبرمجيات."**

## 5. Visual style
- Apple-inspired minimalism: oversized headlines and generous white space.
- Frosted glass (`backdrop-blur`), soft shadows and 24–40 px radii.
- An 8-pt spacing grid and a 1240 px max content width.
- Motion: fade and blur reveals on scroll, a 3D glass mark in the hero, and smooth inertial scroll. Reduced-motion is always respected.
- Product imagery: code-drawn UI illustrations or real screenshots on navy/blue gradient plates.

## 6. Applications
Stationery (letterhead, envelope, business cards), lanyard and ID, notebook, mug and tote. See `public/brand/brand-identity-mockup.webp`.
